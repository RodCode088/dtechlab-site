## 2026-06-28 — Adopt "Red de Datos" logo

**Decision**: Replace the "4 Blocks" logo with the "Red de Datos" (4 connected nodes)
as the sole official logo of DTechLab Studio.

**Reason**: New logo better represents the studio's focus on data, connectivity,
and digital architecture.

**Impact**:
- 57+ logo instances replaced across all HTML and JS files
- Old logo archived in `brand/logos/deprecated/`
